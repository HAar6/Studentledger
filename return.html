Digital Goods & Software License Return Terms

The Study Ledger sells digital software licenses delivered electronically. This policy outlines how digital product returns, license deactivations, and exchanges are handled.

1. Nature of Product & Non-Physical Returns

Because The Study Ledger is an online study productivity software application, there are no physical goods or parcels to be physically returned via post or courier. All returns are processed digitally through our licensing server.

2. Return / Deactivation Window (7 Days)

We offer a 7-day return window from the date of license purchase. If the software does not suit your study routine or meet your expectations within 7 days of subscribing:

  • You may request a digital return and license deactivation.
  • Upon receipt of your return request, your software license key will be deactivated from our server.
  • A 100% full refund will be immediately initiated in accordance with our Refund Policy.

3. Plan Exchanges & Upgrades

If you purchased the Monthly Pass (₹39) and wish to exchange or upgrade your license to the Annual Exam Pass (₹299):

  • We offer 100% pro-rata value adjustment so you don't pay twice.
  • Contact our support team to seamlessly convert your monthly plan to the annual license.

4. How to Request a Return

To return your digital software license, simply send an email with your purchase details:

  • To: harshity16@gmail.com
  • Subject: License Return Request - [Your Name]
  • Include: Registered student email and 12-digit UPI UTR / Razorpay Payment ID.

Our support team will process your return within 24 to 48 hours and issue confirmation.
