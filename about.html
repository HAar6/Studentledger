Chartered Accountancy is one of the world's most demanding curriculums. The Study Ledger was built to eliminate this problem with automated, scientific spaced repetition.

Who We Are

The Study Ledger is an educational study management software operated by KUNDAN TRADING COMPANY based in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India.

Frustrated by cumbersome spreadsheets and distracting mobile study apps filled with notifications and ads, KUNDAN TRADING COMPANY engineered a quiet, vintage digital ledger crafted specifically for CA Intermediate exam candidates.

What the Software Delivers

  • All 6 ICAI Papers Pre-Loaded: Complete structured chapter breakdown for Advanced Accounting, Corporate Laws, Taxation, Costing, Auditing, and FM & SM.
  • The 7-Interval Repetition Engine: Automatically schedules your revisions on Day 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, and 90 so you never forget a provision.
  • Deep Focus Pomodoro Clock: Integrated study timer with 25m, 45m, and 60m focus blocks.
  • 100% Privacy & Zero Ads: Clean, distraction-free Moleskine paper aesthetic that feels like writing in a personal physical ledger.
